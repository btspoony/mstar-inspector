- **The operator console now shows the review duration distribution that justifies the attempt cap.** A new read-only report on the runtime page states its 30-day window and sample size, shows p50 / p90 / max for the end-to-end duration and for the three disjoint phases (queue wait, execution, publication or recovery), and reports what the percentiles exclude as five separate counts — an attempt enters a percentile only when its settlement instant is written and that settlement recorded the publication obligation as discharged — and, where the attempt has a frozen Check, only when that Check reads terminal no later than that instant — so the excluded set names a Check not proven closed at settlement, a settlement with no completion record, an attempt without a usable boundary (no launch row, no durable result, no queue stamp, or out-of-order boundaries), an attempt that never settled, or a definite create rejection that stopped before prepare. An empty window reads as an empty sample, never as zero. Typing a candidate cap shows how many observed attempts it would exclude, their share, and which phase first passed it; the report states that a capped attempt is terminal and that billing for a cap-stopped paid review is still an open question. The card also states, beside the counts, which failure shape is excluded (a launch whose paid step failed without a durable result) and that a sample is measured because its settlement recorded the publication obligation as discharged, delivery idempotency not being part of the interval. The queue send stamp is now copied onto the attempt's handoff row at admission, and a successful attempt records its publication settlement, so both ends of the third bucket come from the attempt's own durable facts.

- **运营控制台新增审查时长分布报告，为时长上限的选择提供依据。** 运行时页面的只读报告会说明其 30 天窗口与样本量，给出端到端时长以及三个互不重叠阶段（队列等待、执行、发布或恢复）的 p50 / p90 / 最大值，并以五个独立计数说明哪些尝试未计入百分位——只有结算时点已写入、且该次结算把发布义务记为已解除的尝试才进入百分位——尝试若有冻结的 Check，还须该 Check 不晚于该时点得出结论——因此被排除的一律注明原因：结算时 Check 未被证明关闭、没有结算记录、没有可测量的边界（缺 launch 行、没有持久结果、缺队列时间戳或边界乱序）、从未结算，或在 prepare 之前就终止的创建拒绝。空窗口显示为空样本，绝不显示为 0。输入候选上限后，报告会给出会被排除的已观测尝试数量、占比，以及首个越过上限的阶段；报告同时说明被上限终止的尝试即为最终状态，且被终止的付费审查是否计费仍无定论。卡片还会在计数旁说明被排除的失败形状（付费步骤失败且没有产出持久结果的 launch 行），以及「样本可测是因为结算把发布义务记为已解除」，投递幂等性不属于该区间。队列发送时间戳现在会在准入时复制到尝试的交接行，成功尝试也会记录其发布结算，因此第三个阶段的起点与终点都来自尝试自身的持久事实。
