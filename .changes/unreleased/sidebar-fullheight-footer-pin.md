- **Full-height sidebar, pinned footer.** The dashboard sidebar now spans the full viewport height, so the Platform-operations footer is viewport-pinned: on a page taller than the viewport the footer is no longer buried at the end of the scroll and stays reachable at the top.

<!-- CN -->
- **撑满视口的侧栏与固定的页脚。** 仪表盘侧栏现在撑满视口高度，因此 Platform-operations 页脚固定在视口内：在高于视口的页面上，页脚不再埋在滚动末尾，在页面顶部即可触达。
