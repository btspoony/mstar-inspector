- **A provider refusal that means "the account is out of credit" no longer reads as a broken provider.** Key verification classified only 401/403 as `invalid_key` and turned every other non-2xx into `unexpected_response`, so a spent account (402, or a 4xx whose error body names credits/quota/billing) told the operator "The provider returned an unexpected response — nothing was stored". The three verify paths — built-in models endpoint, built-in probe and custom-provider probe — now share ONE classifier: 401 stays `invalid_key`, 402 becomes `quota_exhausted`, and a rejection whose body names a spent account becomes `quota_exhausted` as well. A 403/429 has to carry the NARROW signal (`insufficient_quota`, `billing`, `balance`, `payment required`, `insufficient funds`) because a bare "quota" there usually only means the check was throttled; every other non-2xx is judged on the wider credit/quota/billing words. So an ordinary rate-limited 429 still reads as `unexpected_response`, and a 403 without account-spent wording stays `invalid_key`. That reason reaches the settings form as its own structured value, and its copy now says the account is out of quota or credit, that nothing was stored, and that the key itself may still be valid — in both locales. The body read happens only on the failure path, stops after the first 512 bytes, and keeps the bytes already delivered when the stream breaks mid-read.

- **表示“账户余额或额度耗尽”的 provider 拒绝，不再被报告成 provider 异常。** 密钥验证此前只把 401/403 归类为 `invalid_key`，其余所有非 2xx 一律变成 `unexpected_response`，因此账户额度耗尽（402，或错误体指明 credits/quota/billing 的 4xx）会告诉操作者“provider 返回了意外响应 — 未存储任何内容”。内置 models 端点、内置探活与自定义 provider 探活这三条验证路径现在共用一个分类器：401 仍为 `invalid_key`，402 变为 `quota_exhausted`，错误体指明账户已耗尽的拒绝同样变为 `quota_exhausted`。403/429 必须命中窄信号（`insufficient_quota`、`billing`、`balance`、`payment required`、`insufficient funds`），因为那里的 “quota” 通常只说明本次调用被限流；其余非 2xx 才按更宽的 credits/quota/billing 词面判定，因此普通的限流 429 仍为 `unexpected_response`，没有账户耗尽措辞的 403 仍为 `invalid_key`。该原因以其独立的结构化值到达设置表单，文案会说明 provider 账户额度或余额已耗尽、未存储任何内容、且密钥本身可能仍然有效（中英双语）。错误体只在失败路径上读取，最多读前 512 字节，且读取中途断开时保留已收到的字节。
