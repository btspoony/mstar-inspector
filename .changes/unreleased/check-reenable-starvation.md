- **Check recovery** no longer starves a repaired App: the suspended-Check re-enable scan pages past pairs whose credential is unchanged or whose review is still paused, so a repaired pair that sorts after a full page of them is re-enabled instead of staying suspended forever.

<!-- CN -->
- **Check 恢复**不再饿死已修复的 App：挂起 Check 的重新启用扫描会翻页越过凭证未变或仍暂停的 pair，排在一整页这类 pair 之后的已修复 pair 会被恢复，而不是永久挂起。
