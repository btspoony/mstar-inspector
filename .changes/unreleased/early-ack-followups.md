- **A stale duplicate no longer swallows the newest commit, and a skipped delivery no longer reports `neutral` over a review that failed the gate.** The pre-claim ack now compares the head it was keyed on with the PR's live head (the App ledger watermark): a head that has since moved falls through and the round reviews the live commit — in `manual` mode a push queues no review, so that delivery is the newer commit's only chance. The read runs only on a hit and is compared with the head the hit was keyed on (never with itself); an unreadable watermark keeps today's ack with one warning, never a second paid review. A moved head also settles the run this delivery attached for the old head instead of leaving it in progress. A skipped delivery (idempotency hit, publication-journal hand-off, guard refusal, paused App) now settles from the head's own published result against the App's current gate — the confirmed publication's proof first, its `reviews` row when that projection lags — `success` when it meets the gate, `failure` when it misses, and `neutral` only when there is nothing to inherit: no proof and no readable review, an unreadable gate, or a result without a readable score. A `prepared` journal row stays a hand-off to the recovery lane and is not treated as proof the review is visible.

- **过期的重复投递不再吞掉最新提交，被跳过的投递也不再在审查未过闸时报 `neutral`。** 占用前的 ack 现在会把命中所用的 head 与 PR 的实时 head（App 账本水位）比对：head 已前进则不再 ack，本轮直接审查最新提交——`manual` 模式下 push 不会入队审查，这次投递就是该提交唯一的机会。该读取只在命中时发生，并与命中键所用的 head 比对（绝不与自身比对）；水位不可读时保持原有 ack 并记一条 warning，绝不产生第二次付费审查。head 已前进时，本次投递为旧 head 附着的 Check 也会被收尾，不会一直停在 in-progress。被跳过的投递（幂等命中、发布日志交接、guard 拒绝、App 暂停）现在按该 head 自身已发布的结果对照 App 当前闸门收尾：先读已确认发布的证明，投影滞后时再读 `reviews` 行——过闸 `success`、未过闸 `failure`；只有无从继承时才回落 `neutral`——既无发布证明也无可读审查、闸门不可读、或结果缺可读分数。`prepared` 日志行仍只是把投递交给 recovery，不代表审查已可见。
