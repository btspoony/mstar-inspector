- **A recheck round is fitted to the seat's context budget instead of being skipped whole**: the seat's ask is now sized to `review.recheck.assignment_max_bytes` (absent-row default 65,536 B, range 8,192–262,144 — still the only adjustable cap). Previously a long-lived pull request whose open findings plus discussion exceeded that budget produced `skipped: budget (config)` on every round, so no finding was re-assessed and the conversations of already-addressed findings never resolved. Now the findings ride in rotation order until the budget is reached: the rows that fit are sent, every remaining row is accounted `budget` (never sent, never attempted) and keeps its place in the rotation, so the next round prefers it and no row starves. A single finding whose own body exceeds the whole budget still ships, with its original body bounded and marked `[... body truncated ...]` — row id, title, file path and line anchors unchanged — and a thread whose finding was not sent is never offered for resolution. A round that no fit can reach sends nothing, starts no seat and records no failure, exactly as before.

- **recheck 轮次改为按座位上下文预算拟合，不再整轮跳过**：座位收到的材料现在按 `review.recheck.assignment_max_bytes`（无配置行时默认 65,536 B，范围 8,192–262,144，仍是唯一可调上限）裁剪。此前长寿命 PR 的未决 finding 加讨论一旦超过该预算，每一轮都是 `skipped: budget (config)`，没有任何 finding 被重新评估，已被标记为已修复的 finding 的对话也就永远无法收敛。现在 finding 按轮转顺序装入预算：能装下的行被发送，其余行记为 `budget`（未发送、未尝试）并保留轮转位置，因此下一轮优先轮到它们，不会有行被饿死。单个 finding 自身正文超过整个预算时仍会发送，其原始正文被限长并标记 `[... body truncated ...]`——行 id、标题、文件路径与行号锚点保持不变；未发送行的对话线程不会再被提议解决。任何预算都装不下一个目标时仍然不发送、不启动座位、不记录失败，与之前一致。
