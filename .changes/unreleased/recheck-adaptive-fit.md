- **A recheck round is fitted to the seat's context budget instead of being skipped whole**: the seat's ask is now sized to `review.recheck.assignment_max_bytes` (absent-row default 65,536 B, range 8,192–262,144 — still the only adjustable cap). Previously a long-lived pull request whose open findings plus discussion exceeded that budget produced `skipped: budget (config)` on every round, so no finding was re-assessed and the conversations of already-addressed findings never resolved. Now the findings ride in rotation order until the budget is reached: the rows that fit are sent, every remaining row is accounted `budget` (never sent, never attempted) and keeps its place in the rotation, so the next round prefers it and no row starves. A finding that does not fit whole still ships when it heads the rotation — with its original body bounded and marked `[... body truncated ...]` and as much of its own evidence as still fits, row id, title, file path and line anchors unchanged — so an oversized head can never come back as a permanent `budget`; a row behind it that does not fit waits its turn and is rescued the same way once it reaches the head. A thread whose finding was not sent is never offered for resolution. A round that no fit can reach sends nothing, starts no seat and records no failure, exactly as before.

- **recheck 轮次改为按座位上下文预算拟合，不再整轮跳过**：座位收到的材料现在按 `review.recheck.assignment_max_bytes`（无配置行时默认 65,536 B，范围 8,192–262,144，仍是唯一可调上限）裁剪。此前长寿命 PR 的未决 finding 加讨论一旦超过该预算，每一轮都是 `skipped: budget (config)`，没有任何 finding 被重新评估，已被标记为已修复的 finding 的对话也就永远无法收敛。现在 finding 按轮转顺序装入预算：能装下的行被发送，其余行记为 `budget`（未发送、未尝试）并保留轮转位置，因此下一轮优先轮到它们，不会有行被饿死。装不下的 finding 位于轮转队首时仍会发送：原始正文按剩余空间限长并标记 `[... body truncated ...]`，自身证据切片尽量保留，行 id、标题、文件路径与行号锚点保持不变——因此超大的队首行不会永远停留在 `budget`；其后的行本轮装不下则等待下一轮，轮到它成为队首时同样会被救援。未发送行的对话线程不会再被提议解决。任何预算都装不下一个目标时仍然不发送、不启动座位、不记录失败，与之前一致。
