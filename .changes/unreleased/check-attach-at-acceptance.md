- **The review Check attaches when the delivery is accepted, and closes right after the verdict.** The Check attempt is now claimed and its `in_progress` run created on the delivery path — before `REVIEW_QUEUE.send`, not inside the consumer's admission block — so the attach no longer waits for the queue and a cold sandbox, and an admitted message finds a released row instead of racing its creator. The webhook waits at most 2.5 s for the attach; past that the enqueue proceeds and the attach finishes on the invocation context, where the consumer takes over a delivery holder that has been silent for 5 s (a holder still fresh at admission is left alone). The admitted message adopts the row through the same fenced claim (epoch bump, execution deadline narrowed to the 900 s execution window) and terminalizes the run inline from its handle; an adoption that lands after the begin budget releases the row again rather than returning a handle nobody awaits. Measured on staging before this change: attach lag was the queue delay — 10 s, 3 m 20 s, 16 m 45 s and 15 m 31 s across four pull requests — and post-verdict close took 122 s and 113 s, because 69 of 85 attempts in seven days recorded `terminalize_outcome = 'no-handle'`: the consumer never held a handle, so its step-12 terminalize could never run. A refused attach (no Checks surface, a token/GitHub refusal, a failed run-id write) is one redacted `check_attach_failed` warn and changes neither the `accepted` answer nor the enqueue; a duplicate delivery, a queue redelivery or a synchronize event for the same head still creates no second run. A row nobody ever adopts is closed by the Check reconciler when the attach window (one hour) expires, with the existing `expired` conclusion.

- **审查 Check 在投递被接受时挂载，并在给出结论后立即收口。** Check 尝试现在在投递路径上认领并创建 `in_progress` 运行——在 `REVIEW_QUEUE.send` 之前，而不是位于消费方的准入块内——因此挂载不再等待队列与冷启动沙箱，被准入的消息也会找到一条已释放的行，而不是与创建者抢跑。webhook 最多等待挂载 2.5 秒；超过后照常入队，挂载在调用上下文上继续完成，而消费方可以接管已静默 5 秒的投递持有者（准入时仍然新鲜的持有者不会被抢占）。被准入的消息通过同一套带围栏的认领接管该行（epoch 递增，执行截止收窄为 900 秒执行窗口），并用自己的 handle 就地收口该运行；若接管发生在 begin 预算耗尽之后，则把行重新释放，而不是把一个无人等待的 handle 返回出去。改动前的预发环境实测：挂载延迟等于排队延迟——四个 PR 分别为 10 秒、3 分 20 秒、16 分 45 秒与 15 分 31 秒；给出结论后的收口耗时 122 秒与 113 秒，因为七天里 85 次尝试中有 69 次记录为 `terminalize_outcome = 'no-handle'`：消费方从未持有 handle，其第 12 步的收口因此根本无法执行。被拒绝的挂载（没有 Checks 面、token/GitHub 拒绝、run id 写入失败）只产生一条经脱敏的 `check_attach_failed` warn，既不改变 `accepted` 应答也不影响入队；同一 head 的重复投递、队列重投或 synchronize 事件仍不会创建第二次运行。始终无人接管的行由 Check 协调器在挂载窗口（一小时）到期时以既有的 `expired` 结论关闭。
